Concert Hall sound pack for Starbloom Orchestra (starbloom_orchestra)
「音乐厅音源」资源包：本模组乐器使用的第三方录音采样

This resource pack replaces the mod's synthesized instrument samples with recordings. The files under
assets/starbloom_orchestra/sounds/instrument/ and the sample maps under assets/starbloom_orchestra/instruments/ in this pack
are derived from the sample libraries below. They are not covered by the mod's MIT license.
本资源包把模组自带的合成乐器音换成录音。包里 assets/starbloom_orchestra/sounds/instrument/ 下的声音文件与
assets/starbloom_orchestra/instruments/ 下的采样表取自下列采样库，不适用本模组的 MIT 协议。

Changes / 修改:
  Mixed down to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz, silence before the attack trimmed, decaying notes cut and faded
  out, loop points and loop crossfades added to sustained notes, levels adjusted, re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.
  Only some notes and velocity layers are used. See docs/INSTRUMENT_SAMPLES.md and tools/instrument_sources.py
  in the source repository.
  混成单声道、重采样到 44.1 kHz、切掉起音前的静音、衰减的音截短并淡出、持续音加了循环点与交叉淡化、调整了音量，
  重新编码为 Ogg Vorbis；只用了一部分音与力度层。做法见源码仓库的 docs/INSTRUMENT_SAMPLES.md 与 tools/instrument_sources.py。
